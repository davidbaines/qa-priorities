    Order comes from your choices (a comparison sort — every item is distinct unless you marked a pair equal). Score is a Bradley–Terry strength estimate from the same choices, smoothed to respect your order and shifted so the lowest reads at least 1; read the gaps between bars as rough confidence, not exact distance. Tied scores mean two items were too close for the data to separate. Checked items are hidden by default.
